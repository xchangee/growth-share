# Community code of conduct

Growth Share is intended to be a welcoming, practical project for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Give context, acknowledge uncertainty and make room for questions.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Help keep examples accessible to people who do not share your language, tools or environment.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory, sexualized or demeaning comments or imagery.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting real employee or attendee data, unlicensed portraits, credentials or confidential company material.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, events and other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban participants when necessary and proportionate.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the same private channel described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be handled with discretion, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where action or law requires disclosure. Maintainers involved in a report must recuse themselves from deciding it where reasonably possible.

Good-faith disagreement is not misconduct. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this code.
